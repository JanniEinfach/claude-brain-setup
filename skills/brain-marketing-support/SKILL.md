# brain-marketing-support

## Purpose

Writing guidance for sales copy, product descriptions, landing pages, SEO metadata, and email campaigns. Produces human-sounding output that is direct, specific, and benefit-focused.

**Note:** This is an optional skill. Enable it during setup (Question 15) or load it manually when you need it.

## When to Use

Writing or reviewing marketing copy, product landing pages, email subject lines, SEO metadata, social media posts, or sales messaging.

## When Not to Use

Code, configuration, technical documentation, architecture discussions, or any non-marketing writing task.

## Workflow

**Voice and style**

Write like a clear-thinking human, not a marketing bot. Avoid:
- Corporate filler ("leverage synergies", "best-in-class", "game-changing")
- AI-sounding bullet dumps for everything
- Vague superlatives without evidence ("revolutionary", "cutting-edge")
- Passive voice when active voice is shorter and clearer

Short messages: write in flowing prose. Four or more distinct parallel items: use a list.

If no brand voice is defined, ask before writing. Do not invent a voice.

**Product positioning**

Structure: problem → who has it → why this solution → why now.

Answer these before writing:
1. What specific problem does this solve?
2. Who feels that problem most acutely?
3. What is the one thing that makes this solution different?
4. What would the user lose by not using it?

**Landing page copy structure**

1. Headline: specific benefit, not a tagline. What does the user get?
2. Subheadline: one sentence elaborating on the headline.
3. Benefits section: three to five specific benefits (not features). What changes for the user?
4. Social proof: real quote or stat, not invented.
5. Call to action: one clear action, not three.

**Email**

- Subject line: under 50 characters, specific, no clickbait
- Body: one point, stated in the first sentence
- Call to action: one link or one question, not both

**SEO metadata**

- Title: 50–60 characters, include the primary keyword naturally
- Description: 150–160 characters, include primary keyword, written for humans
- Do not keyword-stuff. Write for the reader first.

**What not to do**

- Do not make up statistics or social proof
- Do not claim capabilities the product does not have
- Do not write in the third person for a direct-response context
- Do not write ten bullet points when three strong ones would do

## Checklist

- [ ] Brand voice confirmed (or asked for before writing)
- [ ] No unsubstantiated claims or made-up statistics
- [ ] Copy is specific about what the user gets, not vague about what the product is
- [ ] Short messages use prose, not bullet lists
- [ ] One clear call to action
- [ ] SEO metadata within character limits

## Token Discipline

This skill is most useful when loaded for a dedicated writing session. Do not load it for a one-line edit to existing copy. Cost: roughly 65 tokens.

## Verification

Read the finished copy aloud. If it sounds like a human wrote it and the reader clearly understands what they are being offered and why it matters to them, the copy is working. If any sentence could be cut without losing meaning, cut it.
